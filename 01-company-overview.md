# Company Overview

## Organization

**FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.** is a fictional financial technology (FinTech) company that provides secure digital financial services to individuals and small businesses.

The organization offers:

- Online banking
- Mobile payment services
- International money transfers
- Digital wallets
- Merchant payment solutions

The company operates primarily through cloud-hosted applications, web services, and mobile platforms, making cybersecurity critical to maintaining customer trust, regulatory compliance, and business continuity.

---

# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. are to:

- Protect customer financial information
- Maintain service availability
- Prevent fraud and cyberattacks
- Meet regulatory and compliance requirements
- Ensure business continuity
- Protect company reputation

---

# Security Objectives

To support its business goals, the organization aims to:

- Protect confidential customer data
- Ensure system availability
- Detect and respond to security incidents
- Reduce cybersecurity risks
- Strengthen identity and access management
- Improve employee security awareness
