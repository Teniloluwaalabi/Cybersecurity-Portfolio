# Incident Timeline – FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.

## Incident ID

**FINS-IR-001**

## Incident Title

**Suspected Employee Credential Compromise Through Phishing**

## Incident Status

**Under Investigation / Response in Progress**

## 1. Purpose

This timeline documents the major events and response actions associated with the simulated phishing-related credential compromise.

Maintaining an incident timeline helps the response team understand what happened, when actions were taken, and how the incident progressed from initial detection through containment and recovery.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional cybersecurity portfolio exercise and does not represent a real security incident.

## 3. Key Response Milestones

### Detection

The incident began with the identification of a suspicious phishing communication and subsequent indicators of unusual authentication activity.

### Classification

The incident was classified as **High severity / P1 priority** because compromised credentials could potentially provide unauthorized access to business systems and sensitive information.

### Containment

Containment actions included:

* Restricting the affected account
* Revoking active sessions
* Resetting credentials
* Reviewing MFA
* Restricting suspicious access
* Preserving relevant investigation information

### Eradication

The response team reviewed the compromised account and related access to identify and remove conditions that could allow continued unauthorized activity.

### Recovery

Recovery activities included verifying account security, restoring appropriate access, and applying enhanced monitoring.

### Post-Incident Review

The incident was reviewed to identify security weaknesses and opportunities to improve phishing prevention, authentication controls, security awareness, and incident response procedures.

## 5. Timeline Observations

The timeline demonstrates several important incident-response principles:

1. Early detection can reduce the potential impact of an account compromise.
2. High-risk incidents should be escalated quickly.
3. Account containment should occur before unauthorized access can continue.
4. Evidence should be preserved throughout the investigation.
5. Recovery should include verification rather than simply restoring access.
6. Post-incident review is necessary to identify preventive improvements.

## 6. Final Timeline Assessment

The simulated response followed a structured progression from **detection and classification through containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review**.

The timeline provides a documented sequence of response activities and supports the final incident report and lessons-learned assessment.
