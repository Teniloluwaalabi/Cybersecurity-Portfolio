# GRC Gap Analysis – FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies gaps between FinTrust's hypothetical current security controls and the desired security practices referenced in the GRC assessment.

The objective is to prioritize weaknesses, understand their potential business impact, and identify areas requiring corrective action.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional educational assessment. The findings are hypothetical and do not constitute a formal audit, regulatory assessment, or certification decision.

## 2. Assessment Method

The gaps below are derived from the fictional control assessment and framework mapping completed in this project.

Each gap is evaluated using:
- **Gap severity:** The significance of the identified weakness.
- **Potential impact:** The possible effect on confidentiality, integrity, availability, or business operations.
- **Priority:** The urgency of addressing the gap.

These ratings are preliminary portfolio assessments, not measurements from a real organization.

## 4. Detailed Findings

### GAP-01: Inconsistent Multi-Factor Authentication

**Severity:** Critical

MFA is not consistently enforced across corporate and high-risk accounts.

**Risk:** Stolen credentials could be used to access business systems.

**Recommended action:** Enforce MFA, prioritize privileged accounts, and review exceptions.

### GAP-02: Inconsistent Access Reviews

**Severity:** High

Access permissions may not be reviewed consistently against employees' current responsibilities.

**Risk:** Users could retain unnecessary privileges.

**Recommended action:** Establish periodic access reviews and document approval and removal of access.

### GAP-03: Security Awareness Weaknesses

**Severity:** High

Employee training and phishing-awareness activities require improvement.

**Risk:** Employees may be more susceptible to social-engineering attacks.

**Recommended action:** Introduce regular training, phishing simulations, and clear reporting procedures.

### GAP-04: Patch Management Weaknesses

**Severity:** High

A consistent process for prioritizing and tracking software updates requires improvement.

**Risk:** Known vulnerabilities may remain unresolved.

**Recommended action:** Establish patching timelines based on severity, assign owners, and track outstanding updates.

### GAP-05: Limited Security Monitoring

**Severity:** High

Centralized logging and security-alert review require improvement.

**Risk:** Suspicious activity may remain undetected for longer.

**Recommended action:** Centralize important logs, establish alert ownership, and document monitoring procedures.

### GAP-06: Third-Party Risk Management

**Severity:** High

Vendor security reviews are not consistently documented.

**Risk:** Inadequate supplier security could affect FinTrust's systems or information.

**Recommended action:** Classify vendors by risk and conduct appropriate security assessments before and during the relationship.

### GAP-07: Data Protection Validation

**Severity:** High

Encryption practices and access restrictions for sensitive information require further validation.

**Risk:** Sensitive customer or financial information could be exposed.

**Recommended action:** Verify encryption requirements, data-access permissions, and data-handling procedures.

### GAP-08: Backup and Recovery Testing

**Severity:** High

The organization needs a consistent, documented backup-testing and recovery-validation process.

**Risk:** Critical services may take longer to restore after an incident.

**Recommended action:** Test backups and recovery procedures regularly and record the results.

### GAP-09: Security Governance

**Severity:** Medium

Policy review schedules and ownership of security controls require clarification.

**Risk:** Policies may become outdated or controls may not be maintained consistently.

**Recommended action:** Assign policy owners, establish review dates, and track management approval.

### GAP-10: Incident Response Exercises

**Severity:** Medium

Regular exercises to validate incident-response readiness require improvement.

**Risk:** Teams may be unfamiliar with responsibilities or escalation procedures during a real incident.

**Recommended action:** Conduct tabletop exercises and document improvement actions.

## 6. Key Observations

The main improvement areas relate to identity and access management, employee awareness, vulnerability management, security monitoring, and third-party risk.

Several gaps also concern the consistency of documented processes rather than the complete absence of security controls.

Addressing these weaknesses requires assigned ownership, measurable deadlines, supporting evidence, and periodic review.

## 7. Conclusion

The gap analysis translates the control assessment into actionable improvement areas.

The findings will inform the remediation plan, where each gap will be assigned a recommended action, priority, responsible role, and target completion period.
