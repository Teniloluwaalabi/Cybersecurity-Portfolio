# Framework Mapping – FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.

## 1. Purpose

This document maps selected information-security controls assessed at FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. to relevant areas of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) and ISO/IEC 27001.

The objective is to demonstrate how organizational security controls can support recognized cybersecurity and information-security management practices.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional portfolio exercise. The mapping is illustrative, not a formal compliance determination, certification, or legal opinion. References indicate alignment areas and do not establish that FinTrust complies with either framework.

---

## 2. Frameworks Referenced

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

The assessment uses the five core functions:

- **Identify:** Understand assets, business context, and cybersecurity risks.
- **Protect:** Implement safeguards to manage cybersecurity risks.
- **Detect:** Identify potential cybersecurity events.
- **Respond:** Take action regarding detected cybersecurity incidents.
- **Recover:** Restore capabilities affected by cybersecurity incidents.

### ISO/IEC 27001

ISO/IEC 27001 provides requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

This assessment considers relevant information-security management topics and selected Annex A control areas. A detailed control-level conformity assessment has not been performed.

## 4. Mapping Observations

### Governance

Security policies, assigned responsibilities, and management oversight support the governance of information-security risks.

**Improvement needed:** Strengthen policy review, ownership, approval, and exception tracking.

### Risk Management

Asset identification and vulnerability management support the identification and prioritization of cybersecurity risks.

**Improvement needed:** Maintain documented risk decisions and track remediation to completion.

### Access and Data Protection

Access restrictions and authentication controls help protect sensitive information.

**Improvement needed:** Enforce MFA consistently, review privileges, and verify protection of sensitive data.

### Detection and Incident Response

Logging, monitoring, and documented incident-response procedures support the identification and handling of security events.

**Improvement needed:** Improve centralized monitoring and regularly test response procedures.

### Third-Party Risk

Vendor security reviews help identify risks arising from external service providers.

**Improvement needed:** Establish risk-based vendor assessments and periodic reviews.

### Recovery and Resilience

Backup and recovery procedures support the restoration of critical services.

**Improvement needed:** Test recovery procedures and document recovery priorities.

---

## 5. Framework Alignment Gaps

The assessment identifies several areas where stronger implementation evidence would be required:

1. Documented and consistently performed access reviews.
2. Evidence of MFA enforcement across relevant accounts.
3. Training completion and awareness evaluation records.
4. Patch-management records and remediation tracking.
5. Evidence of security-log monitoring and alert review.
6. Documented vendor assessments and risk decisions.
7. Backup restoration and recovery-test records.
8. Approved, reviewed, and maintained security policies.

These are evidence requirements for the fictional assessment, not verified deficiencies at a real organization.

---

## 6. Recommendations

FinTrust should:

- Assign owners to key security controls.
- Maintain evidence of control operation.
- Review control effectiveness periodically.
- Document risks and remediation decisions.
- Track corrective actions through completion.
- Review changes to relevant frameworks and applicable obligations.
- Use internal reviews to identify areas requiring further assessment.

---

## 7. Conclusion

The mapping illustrates how FinTrust's selected security controls relate to recognized cybersecurity and information-security management frameworks.

The assessment highlights opportunities to improve access control, security awareness, vulnerability management, monitoring, third-party risk management, and recovery readiness.

Further evidence gathering and a formal, appropriately scoped assessment would be required before making any conclusion about actual conformity or certification.
