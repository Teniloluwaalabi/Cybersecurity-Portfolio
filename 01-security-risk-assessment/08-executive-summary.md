# Executive Summary

## Purpose

This cybersecurity risk assessment was conducted for **FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.**, a fictional financial technology organization providing online banking, mobile payments, international money transfers, digital wallets, and merchant payment services.

The purpose of the assessment was to identify key cybersecurity risks, evaluate their potential impact on the organization, and recommend appropriate security controls.

> **Disclaimer:** FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. is a fictional organization created for portfolio and educational purposes. This assessment is not a formal audit, penetration test, or certification assessment.

---

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several cybersecurity risks that could affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of FinTrust's systems and information.

The highest-priority risks identified were:

1. **Phishing attacks**
2. **Credential theft**
3. **Ransomware**
4. **Customer data breaches**
5. **Insider threats**
6. **Unpatched systems**
7. **Third-party vendor compromise**

These risks could result in unauthorized access, financial losses, service disruption, exposure of sensitive information, regulatory consequences, and reputational damage.

---

## Critical Risks

### Phishing

Phishing was assessed as a critical risk because successful attacks could result in compromised employee credentials and unauthorized access to organizational systems.

**Recommended controls:**

- Security awareness training
- Phishing simulations
- Multi-factor authentication
- Email security controls
- Suspicious-email reporting procedures

---

### Credential Theft

Compromised credentials could allow unauthorized individuals to access sensitive systems and information.

**Recommended controls:**

- Multi-factor authentication
- Strong authentication requirements
- Role-based access control
- Least privilege
- Access reviews
- Monitoring of suspicious authentication activity

---

### Ransomware

A ransomware incident could disrupt critical financial services and significantly affect business operations.

**Recommended controls:**

- Endpoint protection
- Regular security patching
- Network segmentation
- Encrypted backups
- Recovery testing
- Incident response procedures

---

## Recommended Priorities

### Immediate

FinTrust should prioritize:

- Implementing MFA for critical systems
- Strengthening employee security awareness
- Reviewing privileged accounts
- Establishing formal patch management
- Verifying backup and recovery capabilities

### Short Term

The organization should:

- Improve security monitoring and logging
- Conduct vulnerability assessments
- Establish third-party risk management procedures
- Review security policies
- Conduct incident response exercises

### Long Term

FinTrust should:

- Mature its security governance program
- Establish continuous security monitoring
- Conduct regular risk assessments
- Measure security control effectiveness
- Continuously improve its cybersecurity risk management processes

---

## Framework Alignment

The recommended controls were mapped to relevant areas of the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework** and concepts associated with **ISO/IEC 27001**.

The assessment considers the following NIST CSF functions:

- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

---

## Business Impact

If the identified risks are not appropriately managed, FinTrust could experience:

- Financial losses
- Customer data exposure
- Service disruption
- Loss of customer trust
- Reputational damage
- Regulatory or contractual consequences

Effective cybersecurity risk management can help reduce these risks while supporting business continuity and customer confidence.

---

## Conclusion

The assessment indicates that FinTrust should prioritize identity security, employee awareness, vulnerability management, data protection, security monitoring, backup and recovery, and incident response.

Cybersecurity risk management should be treated as an ongoing process rather than a one-time activity. Regular risk assessments, control reviews, security testing, and continuous improvement should be incorporated into the organization's security governance program.

The recommendations in this assessment provide a starting point for improving FinTrust's cybersecurity posture and establishing a more structured approach to managing information security risk.
