# Cybersecurity Risk Matrix

This risk matrix illustrates how likelihood and impact were used to prioritize cybersecurity risks for FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.

## Risk Calculation

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

- Likelihood: 1–5
- Impact: 1–5

## Risk Rating

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## Risk Matrix

| Likelihood \ Impact | 1 – Very Low | 2 – Low | 3 – Moderate | 4 – High | 5 – Very High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 – Very High | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| 4 – High | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| 3 – Moderate | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| 2 – Low | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| 1 – Very Low | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

## Highest-Risk Areas

The assessment identified the following critical risks:

- Phishing — 25
- Credential Theft — 25
- Ransomware — 20

These risks require urgent attention because they combine a significant likelihood of occurrence with potentially severe business impact.
