# Risk Matrix

## Overview

The risk assessment evaluates identified cybersecurity risks based on their **likelihood of occurring** and their potential **business impact**.

Risk ratings are used to prioritize security improvements and determine where resources should be focused.

### Risk Calculation

**Risk = Likelihood × Impact**

Both likelihood and impact are rated on a scale of 1–5.

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
|------:|------------|--------|
| 1 | Rare | Negligible |
| 2 | Unlikely | Minor |
| 3 | Possible | Moderate |
| 4 | Likely | Major |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Severe |

### Risk Rating

| Score | Rating |
|------:|--------|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

# Risk Prioritization

## 🔴 Critical Risks

### 1. Phishing Attacks

**Risk Score: 25**

Phishing represents a significant threat because employees may unknowingly disclose credentials or interact with malicious content.

**Recommended priorities:**

- Security awareness training
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Email filtering
- Phishing simulations
- Suspicious-email reporting procedures

---

### 2. Credential Theft

**Risk Score: 25**

Compromised credentials could provide attackers with unauthorized access to customer accounts, internal systems, or sensitive information.

**Recommended priorities:**

- Multi-factor authentication
- Strong authentication policies
- Privileged access management
- Login monitoring
- Account lockout and anomaly detection

---

### 3. Ransomware

**Risk Score: 20**

A successful ransomware attack could disrupt critical financial services and cause significant financial and reputational damage.

**Recommended priorities:**

- Regular encrypted backups
- Endpoint protection
- Network segmentation
- Patch management
- Incident response planning

---

# 🟠 High Risks

High-risk areas should be addressed through planned remediation activities and continuous monitoring.

Priority areas include:

- Customer data protection
- Insider threats
- Vulnerability and patch management
- Third-party risk
- DDoS protection
- Malware prevention
- Backup and recovery

---

# Risk Treatment Strategy

FinTrust should use a combination of the following risk treatment approaches:

### Mitigate

Implement security controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.

### Transfer

Transfer some financial consequences of a risk through mechanisms such as cybersecurity insurance or contractual agreements.

### Avoid

Stop an activity where the associated cybersecurity risk exceeds its business value.

### Accept

Accept a risk when it falls within the organization's approved risk tolerance.

---

# Conclusion

The assessment identifies phishing, credential theft, and ransomware as the highest-priority cybersecurity risks facing FinTrust Financial Services Ltd.

Management should prioritize identity security, employee security awareness, endpoint protection, vulnerability management, backup and recovery, and incident response capabilities.

Risk assessments should be reviewed regularly because the organization's threat landscape, technology, and business operations can change over time.
