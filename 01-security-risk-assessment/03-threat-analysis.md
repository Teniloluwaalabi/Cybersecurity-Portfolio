# Threat Analysis

## Overview

FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. operates in the financial technology (FinTech) sector, making it an attractive target for cybercriminals. The organization processes sensitive customer information and financial transactions, making the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its systems essential.

This threat analysis identifies common cyber threats that could impact the organization.

---

# Identified Threats

| Threat | Description | Likelihood | Business Impact |
|---------|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| Phishing Attacks | Attackers trick employees into revealing credentials or installing malware. | High | High |
| Ransomware | Malicious software encrypts company systems and demands payment. | Medium | High |
| Insider Threat | Employees intentionally or accidentally expose sensitive information. | Medium | High |
| Credential Theft | Stolen usernames and passwords allow unauthorized access. | High | High |
| Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) | Attackers overwhelm online services, causing downtime. | Medium | High |
| Malware Infection | Malicious software compromises company devices or systems. | High | Medium |
| Data Breach | Unauthorized access results in the exposure of customer information. | Medium | High |
| Third-Party Vendor Compromise | A supplier or external service provider introduces security risks. | Low | High |

---

# Key Observations

The most significant risks to FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. include phishing, credential theft, ransomware, and data breaches due to the organization's reliance on cloud services, online banking platforms, and digital payment systems.

Implementing employee security awareness training, multi-factor authentication (MFA), endpoint protection, regular backups, and continuous monitoring can significantly reduce these risks.
