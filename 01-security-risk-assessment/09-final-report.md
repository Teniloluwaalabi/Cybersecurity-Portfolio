# 1. Executive Summary

FinTrust Financial Services Ltd. is a fictional financial technology organization providing online banking, mobile payments, international money transfers, digital wallets, and merchant payment services.

This assessment was conducted to identify cybersecurity risks affecting the organization's critical assets, evaluate the likelihood and potential impact of those risks, and recommend appropriate security controls.

The assessment identified phishing, credential theft, ransomware, customer data breaches, insider threats, unpatched systems, and third-party vendor compromise among the key risks requiring attention.

The assessment recommends prioritizing identity and access management, security awareness, vulnerability management, data protection, endpoint security, security monitoring, backup and recovery, incident response, and third-party risk management.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional portfolio exercise. It is not a formal audit, penetration test, compliance assessment, or certification assessment.

# 7. Recommended Controls

The assessment recommends implementing:

### Identity & Access Management

- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Least privilege
- Regular access reviews
- Strong authentication requirements

### Security Awareness

- Employee cybersecurity training
- Phishing simulations
- Suspicious-email reporting procedures

### Vulnerability Management

- Regular vulnerability assessments
- Formal patch management
- Prioritized remediation of critical vulnerabilities

### Data Protection

- Encryption
- Data classification
- Access controls
- Secure data retention and disposal

### Endpoint Security

- Endpoint protection
- Device encryption
- Security updates
- Host-based firewalls
- Centralized monitoring

### Backup & Recovery

- Regular backups
- Encrypted backups
- Restricted backup access
- Restoration testing
- Documented recovery procedures

### Security Monitoring

- Centralized logging
- Authentication monitoring
- Endpoint monitoring
- Security alerting
- Monitoring of privileged activity

### Third-Party Risk

- Vendor security assessments
- Security requirements in contracts
- Periodic vendor reviews
- Incident notification requirements

# 9. Risk Treatment

FinTrust should consider four primary approaches to risk treatment:

### Mitigate

Implement controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk.

### Transfer

Transfer some of the financial consequences of a risk through appropriate contractual or financial mechanisms.

### Avoid

Discontinue an activity when its cybersecurity risk exceeds its business value.

### Accept

Accept a risk when it falls within the organization's approved risk tolerance.

- Conduct regular risk assessments.

# 11. Conclusion

The assessment identified several cybersecurity risks that could affect FinTrust's systems, information, customers, and business operations.

The organization should prioritize identity security, employee awareness, vulnerability management, data protection, security monitoring, backup and recovery, incident response, and third-party risk management.

Cybersecurity risk management should remain an ongoing process. Regular assessments, control reviews, testing, monitoring, and continuous improvement should be incorporated into the organization's overall security governance program.
